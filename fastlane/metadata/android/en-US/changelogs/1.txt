First release.

• A memory map with markers and clustering, on an open map provider with no key.
• A diary: the events of a day, a calendar, and week, month and year views.
• A life timeline, people, places and search.
• Photos, audio and video, with an in-app camera.
• Optional sync with your own Supabase project, and a status for every record.
• Backup and import, and a complete deletion of your data.
• "Near by": what is within a kilometre of you, ordered by distance.
