Why this app

Everything you have lived is worth keeping. This app keeps your memories, your
diary and the events of your life in one place: on a map, and in a timeline that
goes from a day to a week to a month to a year. No account on anyone else's
service is needed to use it. No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no analysis of
your photos or recordings, and no AI writing in your place.

What you can do

• Memories bound to places: a photo, text, a voice recording or a video, with a
  position on the map, and all of it waiting there when you come back.
• A diary: the events of the day with date and time, a month calendar, week,
  month and year views, and an end-of-day note.
• A life timeline: everything in order, newest first, without losing the order
  of a single day.
• People, places and search: attach a memory to the people and places it
  belongs to, and search all of it.
• Open maps: no Google Maps key, no Google Maps billing, and the tile credit on
  screen because the tile hosts require it.
• Optional sync: connect it to your own Supabase project if you want, with an
  explicit sync status per record. The app is fully usable offline.
• Backup and import to a folder you choose, and a complete deletion of your data
  from inside the app.

Privacy first

• Your data lives on your device first, and nothing leaves it unless you turn on
  sync yourself.
• Location is read on demand only and never tracked in the background.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking libraries.
• A service_role key never reaches the app.

Open source

Apache-2.0, with the whole source published. Arabic first: the interface is
Arabic right-to-left, and English is available if you prefer it.

Note: sync requires creating your own Supabase project and entering its URL and
public key, and that project's free-tier limits are what will cap your usage.
